# Shipping and damaged item policy

## Shipping delays
Use the latest shipment status returned by the shipping service. If no shipment record is available or the status is stale, tell the customer that the status cannot yet be confirmed and route the case for review. Do not invent delivery dates.

## Damaged products
Ask for the information required by the store's damage workflow, such as a description and photo reference. Retrieve the order and applicable policy before proposing a replacement or refund. Do not execute a financial or replacement action without the required approval.
