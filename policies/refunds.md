# Refund and return policy

## Refund eligibility
Refunds may be proposed only after the return is received and any required inspection passes. The order must have a payment record and a positive refundable balance. Current order and payment facts must come from system records, not customer assertions or policy text. Every refund requires a reviewer approval before execution.

## Refund processing status
A submitted refund is not the same as a completed refund. Customer-facing updates must reflect the status returned by the payment provider. If the provider outcome is uncertain, do not claim success; reconcile the existing operation before retrying.

## Exceptions and escalation
Escalate cases with missing order records, missing return records, failed inspection, conflicting evidence, uncertain payment state, or no applicable policy. Never infer eligibility from a semantic retrieval score alone.
